MINDFRAME

FOUR SYSTEMS · ONE LIFE

Five waves of enemy Iterations are inbound. You are the only pilot in the space system and you have one ship. Hold the system. You are Earth's last hope.

WASD thrust  ·  MOUSE aim  ·  CLICK / SPACE fire  ·  P pause

ITERATION fires blue pulses. The basic enemy unit.
WATCHER is fast but weak. Tries to ram you.
PRIMARY is the enemy's elite. Strong tanks that will fire large bombs at you.
TRACKER hangs back and launches homing seekers. Outrun them.
OVERSEER carries a spinning shield arc. Shoot it from behind.
SPLINTER bursts into two Watchers when it dies.
THE RULER is the enemy leader. Extremely powerful and can kill you in a few hits.
